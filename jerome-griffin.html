Major projects

Mobile card game

Griffin House of Rooks

Four-player mobile Rook card game developed through HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

  • Four-player trick-taking gameplay, bot players, bidding and auction systems, and dealer rotation
  • Six-card Nest, trump systems, legal-play enforcement, trick resolution, scoring, and redeals
  • Shoot the Moon and full game lifecycle management
  • Responsive portrait and landscape interfaces, mobile-first UX, and custom physical table presentation
  • Card readability, avatars, bidding UI, speed controls, and progression planning
  • Runtime debugging, real-device testing, regression testing, and protected release baselines
  • Isolated candidate builds, evidence packages, and release / deployment gates

A pure-C# Rook engine developed during the project passed a 37-test validation suite. Investigation used Android remote debugging, Chrome DevTools, DOM and computed-style inspection, caching and service-worker checks, screenshots, recordings, source diffs, and physical-phone verification.

Unity / C#

Griffin House Hold’em

Commercial-style Unity and C# Texas Hold’em project using original Griffin House design and assets.

  • Dealing, blinds, betting, dealer rotation, and showdown logic
  • Main pots, side pots, split pots, chip conservation, and all-in handling
  • Bust and rebuy behavior, cash-out and table sessions, bot players, achievements, avatars, and audio
  • Debugged Return to Hand state corruption, premature hole-card display, split-pot behavior, and bust / all-in locks
  • Debugged audio synchronization, missing sounds, ambience volume, and achievement overlays
  • Unity and Blender work on padded rails, felt, card trays, card occlusion, decorative inserts, and layered construction
  • Camera positioning and multiple card-count presentation states

Mobile physics / AR concept

Pool Shot Mapper

Mobile billiards application concept and development project focused on table mapping, shot routes, and physics verification.

  • Pool-table mapping, pocket selection, and automatic or manual pocket choice
  • Ball detection, camera and photo workflows, AR concepts, and walk-around AR concepts
  • Bank-shot and kick-shot calculations, route visualization, and diamond-system assistance
  • Basic / Pro feature separation, shot-speed controls, English and spin concepts, and scratch detection
  • Shot playback, mobile controls, rail selection, remapping, undo, haptics, offline operation, and sharing workflows
  • Physics testing with large automated and randomized simulation runs, including hundreds of thousands of test cases, to find geometry and collision failures

Closing profile

Build it. Measure it. Test it.

Jerome Griffin is a hands-on product creator who combines practical problem solving, visual judgment, persistent testing, and AI-assisted development to turn ambitious ideas into functioning systems.

The strength is not limited to writing code. He defines what the product should do, directs implementation, evaluates the actual runtime, recognizes when the result does not match the intended experience, traces failures through evidence, and drives the project through repeated testing and refinement.

Across Griffin House of Rooks, Griffin House Hold’em, Pool Shot Mapper, and practical engineering projects, the approach stays the same:

Build it. Measure it. Test it. Find what is actually wrong. Protect what already works. Fix the real cause. Verify it on the real device. Then move forward.
