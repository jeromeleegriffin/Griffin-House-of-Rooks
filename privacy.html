House of Rooks privacy

Author: Jerome Griffin

No accounts. No analytics SDK. Your name, avatar, sound, and rule choices stay in this browser’s local storage.

Live tables use a matchmaking connection so phones can find each other. Card play is sent between the seated devices. We do not sell that data.

Offline practice never leaves the phone.

House of Rooks is an independent trick-taking table. Not affiliated with Hasbro.
